**Title:** Red Starbase — drive a Mars rover through a living Martian Starbase (Blender + three.js r169)

**Body:**

Red Starbase is a browser-only interactive art experience: you drive a six-wheeled Mars
rover across a Martian colony inspired by SpaceX's Starbase — orbital launch pad,
Mechazilla tower, propellant farm, habitat domes, greenhouse, solar arrays — and the
whole visit builds toward one payoff: the starship launch you witness from the watch deck.

Art direction is an *Outer Wilds* eternal dusk: violet zenith melting into burnt-pink
horizon, violet shadows / amber highlights film grade, warm base windows and neon light
strips coming on as the sun sets. A 300-second day/night cycle and a weather machine
switch between clear, dust storm and deep night (Milky Way, headlights, starship light
show). Every frame is meant to be screenshot-worthy — the built-in photo mode (P) hides
the HUD and turns the camera into an orbit rig.

Everything is self-contained:

- **13 Blender assets** (rover, starship, tower, domes, tanks, crystal, lander…) are
  generated by one headless Python script — `blender --background --python
  tools/blender/build_assets.py` regenerates every `.glb` in ~40 s. The rover's six
  wheel pivots are Blender empties consumed directly by the physics rig.
- **Terrain, sky, materials, particles, audio are 100% procedural** — no textures, no
  HDRs, no sound files.
- Cinematic post chain: SSAO → DOF → UnrealBloom → custom FINAL pass (radial chromatic
  aberration, 10-step god rays, lens dirt during storms, film grain) → OutputPass.
- Low-gravity driving model with hand-brake slides, terrain-conforming suspension,
  trauma camera shake; shared `surfaceAt()` sampler keeps physics, camera, collisions
  and race gates on the exact same ground.
- Mission line (inspect → repair a leaking tank by holding E → collect 6 samples →
  watch the launch), a 5-gate time trial with local leaderboard, hidden Tesla Roadster
  easter egg, 4 quality presets with auto-downgrade, gamepad-free mobile twin-stick.
- Zero build step required: three.js is vendored, an import map resolves `three`
  natively, so the repo itself is the site.

Built with three.js r169 (WebGL2). Live demo: https://red-starbase-wgmag3xoh66.qoder.website/ · Source: REPO-URL

Controls: W/S accelerate-brake, A/D steer, Space hand-brake drift, E interact,
P photo mode, T storm, N night, R time trial.
