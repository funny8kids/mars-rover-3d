`public/assets/` already sits. The claim to test: does the constant, rather than a post-hoc translate,

WHAT THE THREE FILES SAY
================================================================================

The constant carries the datum. With ASSET_DATUM_Z = {"launch_tower": -2.535} the rebuild (B) matches the
in-library file's own structure — tower_body at y=+0.000, the -2.535 living in the vertices — while the
same script with the override emptied (C) lands 2.535 m higher and puts the difference on the node. That
split is exactly what tools/glb_set_node_y.py used to undo by hand after an export, which is why a rebuild
and the shipped asset were not interchangeable.

  A vs B:     datum (lowest y) delta = +0.000000 m
              top (highest y)  delta = +0.000000 m
              AABB size  = +0.0000 x +0.0000 x +0.0000 m
  C vs B:     verts delta = +0  (a content difference, not a datum one)
              AABB shift = +0.0000 x -2.5350 x +0.0000 m

Content is a separate matter and it stays open. B and A agree on most primitives as vertex SETS and not
on three of them, for a net 6 verts and 200 bytes on the shipped side:

  multiset differs in /launch_tower/tower_body#5: +386 verts unique to the first file, +386 to the second
  multiset differs in /launch_tower/tower_body#16: +60 verts unique to the first file, +60 to the second
  multiset differs in /launch_tower/tower_body#17: +439 verts unique to the first file, +433 to the second
  as multisets: 15/18 shared primitives hold exactly the same vertex set
  verts 129456 vs 129450

So re-exporting launch_tower is a content change, not a byte-for-byte replay of the library: the tower's
silhouette and the pad colliders want a look before public/assets/launch_tower.glb is replaced. That is
the remaining half of #95.

One more reading the same pair gives for free: the two builds of THIS source (the first copied to
/tmp as 462174c1…, the run quoted above as b395d887…) differ in bytes while agreeing on file-space
bounds to 0.000000 m and on the per-primitive vertex counts. That is the residual float drift commit
c9ac99c left deliberately in place after the index-order drift was pinned; it is why reproducibility
here is stated as bounds and counts, not as a file hash.

Reproduce:

  blender --background --python tools/blender/build_base_v2.py -- launch_tower
  python3 tools/glb_datum_probe.py public/assets/launch_tower.glb /tmp/rsb_stage3/launch_tower.glb
