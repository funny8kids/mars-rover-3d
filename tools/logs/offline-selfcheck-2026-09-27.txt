Two disagreements were open against work.txt, both measured on the harness, not on the world:

  1. `watch:deck|-16.000|-17.000|9.000` read live-only.
     Root cause: offline-world.mjs dropped every collider with a `floor` while the probe keeps them and
     labels them (probe-census.js:6, `c.floor === undefined ? 'solid' : 'floor'`). The two sides were not
     the same population, so the diff measured the harness. Fixed by using the probe's test verbatim.

  2. `samples:site6#0..3`, `site7#0..1`, `site8#0..3` — ten discs — read harness-only.
     Root cause: those are the three deliberately buried mineral sites (`masked = si >= 6`,
     props.js:2800). main.js:274 dresses them at boot and `syncSiteWall` (main.js:777) splices a buried
     site's discs OUT of `base.colliders`, the list the solver and the probe read. The harness stopped at
     the build, so it still carried ten walls that the shipped page does not: an invisible wall, which is
     exactly the defect the census exists to catch.
     Fixed by reproducing boot's membership step in buildOfflineWorld(), after the scatter has been
     pushed (live order: buildBase → regrade → createRocks → createStones → dressSample), and reaching it
     through `base.siteWallUp`, so the rule has one host.

  The predicate moved: main.js:766-771 now forwards to `base.siteK`/`base.siteWallUp`, defined in
  props.js beside the ledger whose numbers they read (props.js:3600-3615, exported at the return object).
  A copy on either side of that line would be a second host the offline census cannot see.
